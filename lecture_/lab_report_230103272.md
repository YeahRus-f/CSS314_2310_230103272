### 2.4 Analysis

Two processes produced a small speedup for both tested strides. At four or more processes, performance generally deteriorated. This suggests that multiprocessing and coordination overhead outweighed the benefit of parallel execution for this workload. Karp–Flatt values above 1 at higher process counts should not be interpreted as a literal serial fraction; they indicate performance worse than the idealized scaling model predicts.
